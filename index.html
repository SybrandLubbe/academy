Backend onboarding

Adobe Experience Manager, from the repository up

A short curriculum for junior backend engineers joining an AEM project. It starts where AEM itself starts — the content repository — and builds outward through Sling, OSGi, and the rest of the stack. The navigation on the left is laid out exactly like a real JCR tree, on purpose: you'll be reading paths like this in CRXDE Lite by the end of module two.
